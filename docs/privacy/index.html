This page explains what data is processed when you visit micz.it and its subpages, and what choices you have.

Who is responsible

You can contact me at m@micz.it for any question about this policy or your data.

In short

  • This site sets no cookies. That’s why there is no cookie banner.
  • I count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data.
  • The site has no ads, no comments, no contact forms and no user accounts.

Visit statistics with GoatCounter

GoatCounter is a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service. For each page view it records the page address, the referring site, the browser and operating system, the screen size and the country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored).

GoatCounter does not set cookies or use any other storage in your browser. To tell apart visits, it keeps a hash of the IP address, the browser identifier and a random value in memory for at most 8 hours, and never writes it to disk. The data is stored in aggregate form and is not shared with third parties.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in knowing how many people read the pages and which ones are useful (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

Technical storage (no consent needed)

Hosting and third-party resources

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and may log it for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

One page embeds a YouTube video using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube says it does not store information about you unless you play the video. If you do, Google’s privacy policy applies.

Pages may link to external sites (for example GitHub, addons.thunderbird.net, PayPal for donations). Their own privacy policies apply once you follow those links.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to m@micz.it to exercise them.

Changes

I may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
