Do you remember the old good Eudora statistics? Do you miss them?
Use this addon to bring statistics to your beloved Thunderbird!

There are plenty of features and customization options available in the add-on. You can crunch the statistics for today, yesterday, or for a date range you specify. You can also export the data in CSV format!

Version 3.0 is a complete redesign: new dashboards, a light and dark theme you can switch on the fly, and much faster statistics.

What's new in version 3.0

Today and Yesterday

A new single-day dashboard compares the day with your usual weekday and shows insights on your inbox, the top correspondents and where the mails ended up.

Many Days

A day-by-day chart with the averages, the Inbox Zero calendar, the heatmap, the weekday chart and the comparison with the previous period.

Custom View

Redesigned, with quick date ranges and filter chips. You can still compare two periods and export the data in CSV format.

How old is your inbox

A new card groups the mails still in your inbox by age. The folders in “Where mails ended up” now show their details and open the folder.

Faster

Caching and an optimized folder scan make the statistics much faster. The advanced options let you set the concurrent queries limit and the recent days cache size.

Options

The options page has been redesigned and saves every change automatically.

How does it work?

After installing it, open the statistics tab from the Spaces Toolbar or the button at the top of the window.

Requirements

Thunderbird 140 or later is required.

Privacy

All data processing is performed locally on your device. No data is transmitted, shared, or stored on any external servers or third-party services. This ensures that your information remains private and secure at all times.
